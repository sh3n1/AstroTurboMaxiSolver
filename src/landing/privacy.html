Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your devices. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies.

Who runs it

AstroTurboMaxiSolver is a free, non-commercial personal project, run by one amateur astronomer.

What stays on your device

Your settings, location, alignment, lists, observing log and equipment are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to us: we have no server that could receive them. Removing the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Camera, motion sensors and location

  • Camera: used to find the stars and work out where your telescope points. The pictures are processed on your device and are neither kept nor sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensors: used on your device to move the sky map with your telescope.
  • Location: used on your device to show the sky from where you are. The app asks for each of these only when you first use something that needs it, and you can say no.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Weather forecast: to show clouds and seeing, the app sends your location, rounded to 0.1° (about 10 km), to Open-Meteo. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees your IP address and the pages requested.
  • Two phones: when you link two phones, they talk directly to each other over your local network. Nothing goes through a server. If you turn on syncing with the linked phone on both, your lists, log and equipment are copied between them.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, to keep your devices in sync:

  • You sign in through Google. Google's sign-in page sets its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
  • The app asks only for access to its own hidden folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see, read or change any of your other files.
  • Your lists, observing log and equipment are saved there, in your own Drive, and read back by your other devices. They never pass through a server of ours. The access Google gives the app is kept only in your device's memory, and lasts an hour.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings. To delete the saved data, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → AstroTurboMaxiSolver → Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AstroTurboMaxiSolver's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your own devices, is never shared with anyone, and is never used for advertising.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here, with its date.
