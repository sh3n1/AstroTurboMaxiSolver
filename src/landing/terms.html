AstroTurboMaxiSolver is a free, non-commercial personal project: a telescope finder that runs in your phone's browser. Using it means accepting these terms.

Safety

Never point your telescope or your phone's camera at the Sun. Looking at the Sun through a telescope can blind you instantly.

Take care at night: watch your step in the dark, and keep your equipment secure on its mount.
